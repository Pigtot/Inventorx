Jenga Net Explorer

A ResNet-18 looks at one Jenga block (7.5 × 2.5 × 1.5 cm) and answers two questions: is it flat, on its side, or standing? And where are its 8 box corners? From those corners, the block's known size, and the camera's lens, PnP works out where the block sits in 3D and how it's turned. Pick a test picture and watch which neurons fire.

From picture to idea

These are the 4 neurons in each layer that fired most for this picture. Each layer looks at a coarser grid (56, then 28, 14, and 7 squares across), but every square covers more of the picture and stands for a more complex pattern. Watch the block go from sharp edges to a blurry "block here" signal. Then head B zooms back up and paints heat maps of the block's 8 corners. The middle of each heat map becomes one corner of the blue 3D box.

Inside the network
