xArm VR Teleop

Press A to arm motion (it starts OFF; press A again to turn it off). Hold the right grip and the arm follows your hand. Let go and it stops. Small hand moves are fine and precise; reaching farther moves the arm much more.
Twist your hand while holding the grip: the gripper turns the same amount (it stays pointing down).
Left trigger click: close the gripper on a block; click again to open it.
Press B to turn the camera picture a quarter turn (it's remembered).
