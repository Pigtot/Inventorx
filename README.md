# xArm 6 Engineering Notebook

This is our club's notebook for learning to use the UFactory xArm 6, a robot arm with six joints.
We also use an Intel RealSense D435 depth camera and a Meta Quest Pro VR headset.

## Weeks

| Week | Dates | What we did |
|---|---|---|
| [Week 1](Week01_2026-10-19/week_summary.md) | Oct 19 – 25, 2026 | Connected the arm and camera to the PC, controlled the arm with the VR headset, and trained a program to find Jenga blocks in pictures. |
| [Week 2](Week02_2026-10-26/week_summary.md) | Oct 26 – Nov 1, 2026 | Put a 3D model of the arm into VR, and fixed the headset freezing when VR starts. |

## Where things are

Each week has its own folder, named after the Monday it starts. For example, Week 1 is in `Week01_2026-10-19/`.

| Inside a week folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `week_summary.md` | **Start here.** What we learned, what works, and links to everything else. |
| `2026-10-25_Sun.md` and others | One log for each day we worked |
| `Lesson01_...md` and others | Lesson notes |
| `code/` | The programs we wrote |
| `media/` | Photos, diagrams, and videos |

Other files:
- `_templates/daily_log.md` is the blank form for a new daily log.
- `CLAUDE.md` and `AGENTS.md` are instructions for the AI helpers we use.
